**Mayoral and Landrat elections: Sachsen-Anhalt additions and corrections.**

- Mayoral: Sachsen-Anhalt adds elections of 1997–2009, mostly by-elections, that name only the elected mayor (`n_candidates` and vote counts are `NA`).
- Mayoral: Sachsen-Anhalt dates, codes and winners corrected; runoff winners now carry their birth year; three `person_id`s change.
- Landrat: Sachsen-Anhalt adds the five runoffs of 2007 and Altmarkkreis Salzwedel 2001 and 2008 and Stendal 2005; the 2007 first rounds now include all postal votes.
