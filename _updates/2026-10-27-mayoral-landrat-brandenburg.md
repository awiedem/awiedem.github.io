**Mayoral and Landrat elections: Brandenburg from 1993, Thüringen 1999–2010, party families.**

- Mayoral: Brandenburg now 1993–2026, including ehrenamtliche Bürgermeister (mostly winner-only); Thüringen adds 1999–2010 (no names); Rheinland-Pfalz to 2026.
- New `party_family` / `candidate_party_family`; `party_switch` now compares party families. Bayern mayors before 2026 now have `candidate_gender`.
- Mayor panel: terms end at the next election, a merger or the legal term limit; some `person_id`s changed.
- Brandenburg losing candidates no longer named; Landrat Burgenlandkreis 2007 winner corrected.
