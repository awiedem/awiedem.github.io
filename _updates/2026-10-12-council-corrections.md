**Municipal council elections: corrections.**

- Thüringen 2024: kreisfreie Städte added. Thüringen 2019 Die Linke, Brandenburg 2014 Greens and Berlin 2011 Piraten no longer counted as `other`.
- New `flag_majority_vote` marks councils elected by majority vote (Thüringen, Rheinland-Pfalz).
- Brandenburg `freie_wahler` now BVB / FREIE WÄHLER only; Aachen 2014 and 2020 corrected; voter counts corrected for Luckow 2024 and Strasburg (Uckermark) 2019.
