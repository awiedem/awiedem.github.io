**Mayoral and Landrat elections: Hessen and Baden-Württemberg results from the municipalities, corrections.**

- Mayoral: Hessen adds the 2023–2026 elections the Landesamt never received and the 2026 by-elections, all with full vote counts. Baden-Württemberg has more elections with full candidate results. Where municipal and Landesamt counts disagree, the Landesamt's are used unless the municipality's final notice confirms its own.
- Mayoral and Landrat: a first round that went to a runoff now carries its leader in `winner_party`, `winner_votes` and `winner_voteshare` in every state (Niedersachsen, Hessen and Baden-Württemberg were `NA`).
- Mayoral: misdated and duplicated rounds removed in Baden-Württemberg and Bayern, which corrects a few mayor-panel terms.
